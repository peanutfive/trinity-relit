Drawing instruction

A single small desert rattlesnake seen from the same elevated three-quarter farmhouse game camera as reference. Original restrained semi-realistic HD-2D game animal sprite, isolated on a genuinely transparent background. The animal is alive, loosely coiled low on the warm floor in an S shape, modestly lifted head at the LEFT of the coil pointing slightly upward so face and eye read in silhouette, tail at RIGHT with a short segmented rattle visibly upturned. Natural tawny/grey-olive hide, muted darker saddle markings and a few ivory neck scales, no orange highlights or unnatural black outlines. Anatomically continuous belly from a single head all the way to a single rattle, no extra tails, no split. Non-aggressive, watchful warning posture, no attack, open fangs, wounds, or people. Warm diffuse side light from upper left like the reference, coldbrown thin contact shadow right underneath its coils. Equivalent to a compact 18–23 px wide coil in a 480x288 game scene; prioritize readable head, coiling shape and rattle when small. The whole sprite visible, tightly centered on alpha; no environment, box, lettering, or background. Reference image is purely for how subtle painterly light blends with ochre boards; do not include the reference's floor.
