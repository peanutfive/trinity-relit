Original, figure-free ending environment for Trinity Relit. Its source material and river palette are our previously painted Wabe river and open dory. A second pass raised the wet shoreline into the upper half of the artwork, so bilingual and mobile captions can occupy the lower beach. The dark river has no visible distant bank or baked boat. Normalized to 1280 by 768, displayed at 480 by 288.

Created for use and distribution in this project. All visual references are original project assets.

Drawing instruction

Paint a single ORIGINAL atmospheric cinematic fine-painterly HD-2D narrative game environmental plate at 5:3, inspired by the dark water and warm brushwork in these two of our own existing paintings. This is an EMPTY and deserted river shore. A flat quiet sand spit in the lower foreground, only modest scuffed olive-grey sand, soft fawn-grey highlights, tiny pebbles and wind marks. NO foliage. The water and fog occupy the entire rest of the frame and maintain a clean uncluttered quiet silhouette. COMPOSITION is vital for later actors: the shore curves gently from the LEFT edge at normalized x0 y0.47, toward x0.32 y0.63, and then descends to x0.60 y0.73 and the RIGHT edge at y0.83. Everything above this line is still charcoal-teal/dull storm-grey water, low subdued pewter reflections. An animated dory will arrive later near x0.53 y0.64 with its BOW close to the low shore on its left; therefore paint NOTHING floating on the water and leave that area clear. Opposite shore never visible — veiled completely by a gorgeous layered bank of pale grey fog across the upper part of the painting, no silhouettes, buildings, mountains, trees, landmarks, pier or figures in mist. All faint lines in distance soften into vapour. The foreground is noticeably sharper than the distance, delicate slightly wet sand at shoreline, very fine familiar hand-painted pixel-game texture, contemplative early morning shadow, a hint of warmth in sand to harmonize earlier game art. No boat, no people, no character, no oars, coins, animal, marker, text or UI. No luminous portal, dramatic sun or huge breakers. This empty desertlike shore should say stillness. Fill the frame completely.

Drawing instruction

Compose a NEW, closely matching version of this original EMPTY river shore plate but SHIFT THE SHORELINE HIGH IN THE FRAME for a game with dialogue over the lower foreground. Same wide 5:3 camera, wonderfully restrained teal mist, dark still water and grey weathered sand, absolutely no objects, buildings or creatures. CRITICAL: sand now fills the ENTIRE LOWER HALF of the image. Diagonal waterline should intersect the far left edge at 25% of image height, run through the point 30% across and 46% down, through the central point (50% across, 50% DOWN the picture), out of the RIGHT EDGE at 59% of image height. This gives plenty of good quality warm-grey matte sand covering the bottom half for type to be overlaid later (no writing in the art!). The WATER settles in the MIDDLE-TOP of the frame between upper 21% opaque broad smoky fog and the given waterline; it remains a DARK quiet waterway wide enough for a dory which will be separately animated later. Keep opposite bank completely hidden by mist. Make the dunes or footprints nearly imperceptible, low warm silt at water's edge, soft sand texture foreground, enough horizontal contrast for shoreline to read, very faint warm silver reflection. Current reference shoreline is at bottom, deliberately RAISE it to halfway up the new scene. No sediment ridges obscuring water, no dock, humans, boats, horizon silhouettes, grass, trees, symbols, trail, berries or lettering. Fine painterly photoreal miniature textures of the source. Do not letter or label either plane. Just the EMPTY sandy shoreline, some even mist above and dark water between. Compose the camera facing this much steeper down toward the ground and the meeting point of sand and water.
