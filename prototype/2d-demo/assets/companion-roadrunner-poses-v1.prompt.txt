Drawing instruction

Create an original HD-2D narrative game's THREE-FRAME ANIMATION SPRITE STRIP on a truly transparent background. Use the supplied two existing project-owned running frames as strict identity, material, pose-angle and color references for this same small real roadrunner. This strip contains NEW STANDING poses, three separate full body drawings, all face RIGHT, in THREE EQUAL HORIZONTAL CELLS, ample transparent space around each. All share identical physical scale, wing profile, one raised dark brown crest, distinct amber eye and tiny pale-blue facial marking, long slender tapering glinting graphite bill, dark brown speckled ochre wings and breast, two dark elegant legs and long blue-black tail with buff streaks. Restrained natural palette and fine realistic hand-painted plumage, avoiding heavy outlines. Standing is absolutely different from running: feet are CLOSE underneath the body and actually support the weight; no running, outstretched feet, jumping, flying, sweat, motion lines or flying plumes. Draw footpads on ONE COMMON BASELINE about four fifths canvas height. No floor or cast shadow; true alpha. Frame order: LEFT — alert upright standing ground bird, calm raised head and both feet planted side-by-side, tail behind left rising a little. CENTER — same stationary bird dips its neck down and forward, beak lowered near the ground about to lay an object; legs still planted and torso stays stable, empty bill. RIGHT — investigating the dirt: the body leans slightly forward, same recognizable face, one foot rests firmly, one bent foot pulls a little dirt backward in a short scrape, no drawn particles; bill angled lower than upright but not touching the ground. No ruby, gem, item, dirt, or writing on any sprite. Make each pose equally sized as characters in adjacent cells of a 3-column 1-row wide atlas; no overlaps or pieces cut off. No typography, text, labels, backgrounds, UI, cell lines or border. Reference images set bird identity only.
