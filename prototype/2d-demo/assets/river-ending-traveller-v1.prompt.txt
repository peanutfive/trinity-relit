Original transparent ending poses for the traveler. Source images are our in-game hero sheet and existing original ferry painting. The whole figure is drawn in four poses, each with a measured foot or seat anchor, in a 720 by 240 strip. The loose coin at the end of the fingers was removed for the separately timed handoff. The near side of the original dory is repainted after boarding; shoes and lower hems stay inside the hull.

Created for use and distribution in this project. All visual references are original project assets.

Drawing instruction

Paint an ORIGINAL four-pose, 4-column by 1-row transparent atlas of the SAME adult traveler from OUR supplied game character sheet, now a passenger in a quiet epilogue. Four separately isolated depictions of the SAME man, same face/height/hat/coat/shoes, same elevated three-quarter side view, same perspective and identical anatomical scale; each faces RIGHT. Fedora is midbrown, slightly dented short-brim felt, tan ochre informal long jacket, neutral dark brown-grey trousers, dark leather shoes. First reference is the actor; second is the melancholy cool lighting of the boat, do not paint any boat. Leftmost pose is standing quietly at rest and looking right, silhouette recognizable. Second pose stepping RIGHT onto a boat, one foot raised onto a bench or sill about a shoe height and one hand out for balance, no surface under him. Third pose stands on planted feet, extending his RIGHT ARM toward the RIGHT at chest height, holding out a small dull silver coin at the fingertips to another person who is OUT OF FRAME. Fourth rightmost pose quietly seated on a bench about knee height, side/back 3/4 facing upper RIGHT toward a misty river beyond, a slight pause, feet hanging down naturally, hands folded loosely on lap. Anonymous, understated, not an action hero. For last pose leave lower legs truly present but the BOTTOM of his jacket and his hip should settle at believable seat-height. Render NO SEAT, furniture, boat, terrain, oar, figures, effects, text or background. Bare feet to head full sprite in each cell. Three standing poses share one barefoot-level baseline and seated shoes come down to approximately the same foot-level line. Full original texture, subtle brushwork, soft cool-dark cloth with dim warm side light under old felt hat. Not cartoon or silhouette, no glow, white halo, outlines or cell borders. Clean true alpha with generous transparent gutters, no checkerboard baked. Four distinct poses, 4:1 layout, one row.
